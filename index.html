One — the dog

Not a protocol.
Just a dog.

InkBull has no yield, no vault, and no whitepaper thick enough to prop a door open.

What it has is one bulldog printed like old tattoo flash: a black plate, a red plate, and a few pixels of misalignment — the look of a screen print pulled in a hurry. We left it that way. That is the mark.

It launched through pons, which means the token and its trading pool went live in one transaction with the liquidity locked automatically. Fixed supply, no second mint, nothing held back for a team.

Four — the order

How to buy

  1. Step 01

    Add the network

    Add Robinhood Chain to any EVM wallet. Chain ID must read 4663. If it reads anything else, stop.

  2. Step 02

    Fund it with ETH

    Bridge ETH to Robinhood Chain. Gas is plain ETH — there is no separate gas coin to buy.

  3. Step 03

    Open the token page

    Open pons, paste the contract address above, and check every character against it before anything else.

  4. Step 04

    Swap for $BULL

    Enter an ETH amount. Check the price impact and slippage in the preview, then sign in your wallet.

Two ways people lose money here. Fake token pages that copy the name and image but carry a different contract address — always match the full address. And any prompt asking you to buy a special Robinhood Chain gas token: no such token exists, so that prompt is a scam.

Five — what happens next

Token lifecycle

Stage 01

Create

The token is minted with a fixed supply and its WETH pool goes live in the same transaction. The liquidity is locked automatically.

Stage 02

Trade

Buys and sells run against WETH in that locked pool and move the price. For the first two blocks, launch limits cap what any one wallet can take.

Stage 03

Graduate

Once enough WETH is paired, the launch graduates. Nothing migrates — trading carries on in the same pool. It marks a threshold, not quality.
