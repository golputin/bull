One — the dog

Not a protocol.
Just a dog.

InkBull has no yield, no vault, and no whitepaper thick enough to prop a door open.

What it has is one bulldog printed like old tattoo flash: a black plate, a red plate, and a few pixels of misalignment — the look of a screen print pulled in a hurry. We left it that way. That is the mark.

It launches on a bonding curve through InkyPump, so there is no presale and no private allocation. Everyone buys from the same curve at the same price at the same moment.

Three — the order

How to buy

  1. Step 01

    Add the network

    Add Robinhood Chain to any EVM wallet. Chain ID must read 4663. If it reads anything else, stop.

  2. Step 02

    Fund it with ETH

    Bridge ETH to Robinhood Chain. Gas is plain ETH — there is no separate gas coin to buy.

  3. Step 03

    Open the token page

    Go to InkyPump on Robinhood Chain, paste the contract address above, and check every character against it.

  4. Step 04

    Buy on the curve

    Enter an ETH amount. The preview shows expected tokens, the fee, and price impact. Sign it.

Two ways people lose money here. Fake token pages that copy the name and image but carry a different contract address — always match the full address. And any prompt asking you to buy a special Robinhood Chain gas token: no such token exists, so that prompt is a scam.

Four — what happens next

Token lifecycle

Stage 01

On the curve

Price follows the bonding curve. Anyone can buy or sell at any time. The 2% fee applies to every trade.

Stage 02

The bond

Once the curve raises its ETH target, liquidity migrates into a Uniswap V4 pool paired with WETH. It happens in a single transaction, with no price jump.

Stage 03

On the pool

The curve stops accepting trades and everything moves to the V4 pool. The fee drops to 0.1%. After that, nothing is scheduled.
